AutoMuse AI 🚗✨

AI-Powered Automotive Concept Design Assistant

AutoMuse AI is an AI-powered automotive design platform that transforms creative vehicle ideas into detailed futuristic vehicle concepts. Designed for automotive design students, transport designers, concept artists, and car enthusiasts, AutoMuse AI helps users explore new design possibilities by generating complete vehicle concepts from simple descriptions.

The platform combines artificial intelligence with automotive design principles to create detailed concepts including vehicle identity, design language, specifications, materials, aerodynamics, structure, sustainability, and interior/exterior inspiration.

🌟 Project Overview;

Automotive design often begins with a simple idea — a vision of a vehicle that does not exist yet.
AutoMuse AI helps bridge the gap between imagination and concept development by allowing users to describe their dream vehicle and receive an AI-generated design proposal.
Instead of starting from a blank page, designers can use AI-generated inspiration as a creative foundation for developing new automotive concepts.

🏗️ How It Works;

User enters their vehicle idea and preferences.

The application sends the concept request to the backend.

The backend processes the request using the Gemini AI model.

AI generates a structured automotive concept report.

The frontend displays the generated vehicle concept in an interactive format.
