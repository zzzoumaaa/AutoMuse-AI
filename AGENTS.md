# AutoMuse AI Design Principles & Rules

## Design Philosophy & Visual Rhythm
- **Minimalist & Breathable**: Maintain an Apple/Tesla-inspired automotive aesthetic with generous negative space, intentional section padding, and clear focal hierarchy.
- **One-Glance Readability**: Prioritize information architecture. Use collapsible or tabbed views for deep technical specs so secondary details do not overcrowd the primary visual stage.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Avoid "everything everywhere all at once" UI clutter. Maintain consistent spacing, subtle borders, and balanced typography.
- **Green Accent Palette**: Accent buttons, scrollbars, active tabs, and callout badges use dark forest & emerald green tones (`#10b981`, `#1c2b20`, `#385441`) rather than stark white or arbitrary bright colors.
- **Single-Screen & Responsive Flow**: Keep interactive components (Header, Prompt Panel, Technical Aerodynamics, Portfolio Report) responsive, aligned, and fluid across mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide screens.
